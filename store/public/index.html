      About this demonstration store

      Demo Store is a working demonstration of a small online shop, built for one client and shown here so the design and behaviour can be reviewed in a real browser. It is not a live shop and it is not accepting customers.

      The catalogue, prices, stock levels and shipping options you see are real fields read from the store's own database, so the behaviour shown is the behaviour the finished store would have. Payment is deliberately not connected: the store is wired to a sandbox/test payment account only, and test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 (any future date, any 3-digit code) walks through the checkout.

      Nothing is charged, no card details are entered on this site, no orders are placed, and no goods ship. No cookies are set and no third-party scripts or resources load on any page.

      Disclaimer: this page is a demonstration only. It is not a live shop, no payment can be taken, and nothing will ship.
